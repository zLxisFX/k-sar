# Presentation sources and credits

Project K-SAR's seven-slide presentation uses custom data compositions, screenshots of the [live workstation](https://k-sar.vercel.app), and traceable mission and map datasets. The original K-SAR logo was designed and supplied by Luis Gian Luna and is reproduced without modification.

## Mission imagery and data

- **Calibrated NISAR data:** NASA–ISRO NISAR mission; L-SAR products processed by NASA JPL and distributed by NASA ASF DAAC. L2 GCOV collection DOI [10.5067/NIL2GCOV-V1](https://doi.org/10.5067/NIL2GCOV-V1), acquisitions [21 October 2026, G4404197169-ASF](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/concepts/G4404197169-ASF.umm_json) and [2 November 2026, G4408731464-ASF](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/concepts/G4408731464-ASF.umm_json). Actual HDF5 frequency-A HHHH/HVHV diagonal covariance layers supply calibrated HH/HV gamma-naught power. Authenticated HTTP byte-range reads extracted the required native windows over the basin. Positive finite samples pass the documented subswath, exception and polarization-validity screens. Valid linear power is area-averaged from identical 10 m UTM grids onto the 854 × 998, 30 projected metre EPSG:3857 grid before conversion to dB. Each polarization has 852,283 common valid cells. Slide 5 plots later-minus-earlier HH change from the [science manifest](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/nasa/nisar/science/manifest.json); all 80 bins and their tails are retained. Original browse PNGs, KML overlays and catalog records remain separately labeled references. These 2026 observations are independent of the 2024 Carina context. Backscatter change alone does not establish flooding, displacement or a particular scattering mechanism. Product semantics: [ASF GCOV guide](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gcov/).
- **Sentinel-1 RTC:** contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024. Original observations are ESA/Copernicus Sentinel-1, 10 July and 3 August 2024. Radiometric terrain correction is by Catalyst; hosting and licensing are through Microsoft Planetary Computer. The [RTC collection](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/api/stac/v1/collections/sentinel-1-rtc) declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The rasters were subset, aligned to the 30 m EPSG:3857 grid, and converted to gamma-naught dB; source IDs and processing details remain in the [raster manifest](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/sentinel-1/manifest.json).
- **Copernicus DSM and sampled profile:** produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved. The public GLO-30-F license, applicable notices and liability statement are retained in [data/NOTICE.txt](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/NOTICE.txt) and the [source license document](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-06/copernicus_contributing_mission_data_access_v2_cop_dem_licenses.pdf).
- **Roads, waterways and hospital-tagged features:** © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The graph and derived samples retain their separate [network notices](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/network/NOTICE.txt).

## Visual construction and fonts

The layered map illustration uses the actual aligned Sentinel-1, Copernicus DSM and OSM geometries. Perspective, plane separation and shadows explain the relationship between inputs as visual presentation aids rather than a three-dimensional terrain model. Counts stated on the slide refer to the full directed graph at an assumed 11.6 m EGM2008 water surface. App screenshots preserve the displayed interface and results. No satellite imagery was generated with AI.

The fonts are **Inter**, **Instrument Serif** and **IBM Plex Mono**, each licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: [Inter license](https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt), [Instrument Serif source and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif), [IBM Plex Mono source and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono).

## Editable presentation structure

The PowerPoint retains editable text, shapes, source notes, a native chart containing all 59 transect samples and a native NISAR HH-change histogram. The histogram uses a numeric XY step outline at the exact 80 bin edges, preserving all 852,283 values without smoothed interpolation. The profile chart includes the original DSM, its assumed 0.390 m lowering and an 11.6 m EGM2008 water-surface reference. The independent routing proof retains 301 paired water-level cases. Slide 6 notes carry all 41 file IDs and SHA-256 identities from the verified application export. The PDF is the static companion to the animated PowerPoint.
