# Presentation sources and credits

Project K-SAR's seven-slide presentation uses bespoke compositions, screenshots of the [deployed application](https://k-sar.vercel.app), and traceable mission and map data. The original K-SAR logo was supplied by Luis Gian Luna and is reproduced without redrawing or recoloring.

[Official image source](https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/amf-34e16d97-d8a3-453f-9d3b-77117c841a3f/) · [JPL image-use policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/).
- **NISAR browse images:** NASA–ISRO NISAR mission; L-SAR products processed by NASA JPL and distributed by NASA ASF DAAC. ## Visual construction and fonts

The layered map illustration uses the actual aligned Sentinel-1, Copernicus DSM and OSM geometries. Perspective, plane separation and shadows explain the relationship between inputs; they are presentation effects, not a measured three-dimensional terrain model. Its crop shows only part of the graph. Counts stated on the slide refer to the whole directed graph. App screenshots preserve the displayed interface and results. No satellite imagery was generated with AI.

The fonts are **Inter**, **Instrument Serif** and **IBM Plex Mono**, each licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: [Inter license](https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt), [Instrument Serif source and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif), [IBM Plex Mono source and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono).

## Editable presentation structure

The PowerPoint retains editable text, shapes, source notes and a native chart containing all 59 transect samples. The chart includes the original DSM profile, its assumed 0.390 m lowering and a 10 m EGM2008 water-surface reference. Screenshots, mission imagery and perspective compositions are embedded raster assets. The PDF is the static companion to the animated PowerPoint.

Original application code and third-party datasets retain their separate licenses. Use of mission imagery or data does not imply endorsement by NASA, ISRO, ASF, ESA, Copernicus or OpenStreetMap. Assisted development and reused research artifacts are documented in the [development history](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/docs/development-history.md).
