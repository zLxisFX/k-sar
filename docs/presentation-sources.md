# Presentation sources and credits

Project K-SAR's seven-slide presentation uses bespoke compositions, screenshots of the [application](https://k-sar.vercel.app), and traceable mission and map data. The screenshots were captured from the local production build; the matching public release is checked separately. The original K-SAR logo was supplied by Luis Gian Luna and is reproduced without redrawing or recoloring.

## Mission imagery and data

- **NISAR browse images:** NASA–ISRO NISAR mission; L-SAR products processed by NASA JPL and distributed by NASA ASF DAAC. L2 GCOV collection DOI [10.5067/NIL2GCOV-V1](https://doi.org/10.5067/NIL2GCOV-V1), acquisitions [21 October 2026, G4404197169-ASF](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/concepts/G4404197169-ASF.umm_json) and [2 November 2026, G4408731464-ASF](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/concepts/G4408731464-ASF.umm_json). Original PNGs, catalog records and footprints remain in the [reference bundle](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/tree/main/data/nasa/nisar). Provider KML image bounds support the derived 62 × 145 comparison grid at 200 projected metres; 8,784 cells meet the common browse-coverage mask. The difference map and native histogram use rounded weighted RGB display-code differences in DN, not calibrated backscatter or flood measurements. The [comparison manifest](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/nasa/nisar/comparison/manifest.json) records the complete method and source hashes. These 2026 browse images do not depict the July 2024 event; quantitative GCOV HDF5 analysis remains pending.
- **Sentinel-1 RTC:** contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024. Original observations are ESA/Copernicus Sentinel-1, 10 July and 3 August 2024. Radiometric terrain correction is by Catalyst; hosting and licensing are through Microsoft Planetary Computer. The [RTC collection](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/api/stac/v1/collections/sentinel-1-rtc) declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The retained research derivatives were subset, resampled to a 30 m EPSG:3857 grid, and converted to gamma-naught dB; source IDs and processing details remain in the [raster manifest](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/sentinel-1/manifest.json).
- **Copernicus DSM and sampled profile:** produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved. The public GLO-30-F license, applicable notices and liability statement are retained in [data/NOTICE.txt](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/NOTICE.txt) and the [source license document](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-06/copernicus_contributing_mission_data_access_v2_cop_dem_licenses.pdf).
- **Roads, waterways and hospital-tagged features:** © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The graph and derived samples retain their separate [network notices](https://github.com/zLxisFX/k-sar/blob/main/data/network/NOTICE.txt). ## Visual construction and fonts

The layered map illustration uses the actual aligned Sentinel-1, Copernicus DSM and OSM geometries. Perspective, plane separation and shadows explain the relationship between inputs; they are presentation effects, not a measured three-dimensional terrain model. Its crop shows only part of the graph; the exact threshold's separating edge lies outside that crop. Counts stated on the slide refer to the whole directed graph at an assumed 11.6 m EGM2008 water surface. App screenshots preserve the displayed interface and results. No satellite imagery was generated with AI.

The fonts are **Inter**, **Instrument Serif** and **IBM Plex Mono**, each licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: [Inter license](https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt), [Instrument Serif source and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif), [IBM Plex Mono source and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono).

## Editable presentation structure

The PowerPoint retains editable text, shapes, source notes, a native chart containing all 59 transect samples and a native NISAR display-difference histogram. The profile chart includes the original DSM, its assumed 0.390 m lowering and an 11.6 m EGM2008 water-surface reference. Screenshots and perspective compositions are embedded raster assets. The PDF is the static companion to the animated PowerPoint.

Original application code and third-party datasets retain their separate licenses. Use of mission imagery or data does not imply endorsement by NASA, ISRO, ASF, ESA, Copernicus or OpenStreetMap. OpenAI Codex generated and revised substantial code, processing scripts, tests, documentation and presentation material; Google Gemini contributed architectural, scientific and visual suggestions. Reused source artifacts and processing steps remain identified in the dataset manifests and notices.
